Privacy Policy for Mlivecast

Last Updated: September 1, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the Mlivecast mobile and web applications (the "App"). By using Mlivecast, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information to provide and improve our live streaming, contest participation, and community services:

  • Account Information: When you register, we may collect your name, email address, username, and profile photo.
  • User-Generated Content: Live broadcasts, recorded video sessions, contest entries, comments, and rankings created within the app.
  • Device Permissions:
    • Camera: To enable live video broadcasting and recording.
    • Microphone: To capture voice and audio during live streaming sessions.
    • Storage / Media: To upload profile avatars, thumbnails, and recorded video replays.

2. Third-Party Services

The App uses trusted third-party service providers to power core features:

  • Google Play Services & Firebase (Google LLC): For secure authentication, database hosting (Cloud Firestore), media storage, and crash analytics.
  • LiveKit & Agora: For real-time low-latency audio/video communication and live streaming infrastructure.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide real-time interactive live broadcasts and contest events.
  • To facilitate voting, standings, and ranking calculations.
  • To maintain app security, prevent fraudulent activity, and ensure compliance with community standards.

4. Data Security

We implement industry-standard encryption (TLS/HTTPS) to protect your personal data in transit and at rest. We do not sell your personal data to third parties.

5. Account & Data Deletion

Users have the right to request the deletion of their account and associated data at any time from within the app settings or by contacting our support team.

6. Children's Privacy

Mlivecast is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.
